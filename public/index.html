About the Meeting

The ECOWAS Directors General of Statistics Meeting is the region’s highest level technical forum for coordinating, harmonizing, and modernizing national statistical systems across West Africa. It brings together heads of National Statistical Offices (NSOs), ECOWAS Commission experts, and development partners to advance the ECOWAS Statistical Policy and Vision 2050.
